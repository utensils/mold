- **One colour per model family.** Every model family now has its own colour,
  defined once and shared by the CLI and the Mac and iPhone/iPad apps. `mold
  list` uses it, and Hunyuan3D, which had none, now has one. Older prints that
  recorded a family under a legacy spelling (for example `ltx_video`) now show
  the family's proper name in the native apps.
