- **Library Filter menu on the Mac.** The Library toolbar's media menu is now
  a Photos-style Filter menu. Choose one of Favourites, Photos, Videos, 3D, In
  a Collection, Not in a Collection, Made from a Source, Has Tags, Untagged,
  Portrait, Landscape or Square. You can combine it with a Model Family submenu
  (each family has its colour dot), and Clear Filters resets both. The button
  always shows what is applied, for example "Not in a Collection · FLUX.1".
  Print Info now names a print's family and shows its colour.
