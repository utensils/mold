- **Finished videos leave the native Queue.** On the Mac and iPhone/iPad apps a
  completed or stopped video now drops out of the Queue on its own, like every
  other render, instead of waiting to be dismissed. Failed and interrupted
  videos stay so you can resume or start them again.
- **Steadier Length slider for videos.** Dragging the Length slider on the Mac
  and iPhone/iPad apps no longer flashes "Calculating length…" or "1 clip · one
  video", so the panel no longer grows and shrinks. The clip count appears only
  when the video will really be made from more than one clip.
