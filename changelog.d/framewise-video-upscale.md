- **Durable Framewise video upscale.** Library MP4/MOV/WebM sources can be upscaled frame by frame with the native Real-ESRGAN/Candle engine through HTTP, CLI, and the desktop Library, with pause/resume/cancel, restart-safe checkpoints, primary-audio preservation when codec-compatible, and a new provenance-bearing gallery video. Variable-frame-rate, HDR, subtitles, chapters, multiple audio tracks, and incompatible primary audio are rejected rather than silently changed; temporal flicker may remain.
