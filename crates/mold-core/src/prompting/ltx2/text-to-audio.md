# LTX-2 text-to-audio prompting

Use only for LTX-2 text-to-audio. Describe the audible result rather than a
camera scene: sound sources, sequence, space, distance, dynamics, texture, and
duration. For speech, provide the exact short line and describe voice and
delivery. Separate dialogue, ambience, effects, and music so their roles do not
conflict.

Inspect `mold --help` and the selected host's capabilities for the current
text-to-audio invocation. Verify the returned artifact is audio before reporting
success.
